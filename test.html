Install ILMO App

Install ILMO on your home screen or desktop for a fast, distraction-free experience.

How to add to Home Screen:
🤖 Android (Chrome / Edge / Firefox):
Tap your browser menu (⋮) and select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
🍏 iPhone & iPad (Safari):
Tap the Share button (square with arrow) at the bottom, then tap "Add to Home Screen".
💻 PC & Mac (Chrome / Edge):
Click the Install icon (⊕) on the right side of the address bar.
